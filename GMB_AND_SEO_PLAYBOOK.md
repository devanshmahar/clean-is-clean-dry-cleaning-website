## 4. Keyword-Rich Review Strategy (Google Maps Ranking Hack)

Google Maps ranks businesses higher when customer reviews and owner responses contain target keywords.

### How to Ask Customers for Reviews:
Send this WhatsApp message after delivery:
> *"Hi [Name], your freshly cleaned clothes have been delivered! Could you take 30 seconds to rate Clean Is Clean on Google? If you mention what you cleaned (e.g. suits, sarees, or wash & fold), it helps our local family business grow! [Your GMB Review Link]"*

### Owner Response Template (Boosts Keyword Density):
> *"Thank you [Name]! We are delighted that you loved our **dry cleaning and doorstep pickup service in [City]**. Our team takes pride in delivering crisp **steam ironing** and fresh **wash and fold** right to your doorstep. Looking forward to serving you again!"*

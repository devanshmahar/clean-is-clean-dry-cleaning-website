Free pickup and delivery is provided on all orders with a minimum order value of ₹300. For orders below ₹300, a standard ₹50 nominal delivery charge applies.

Our pickup partner carries an approved digital scale and weighs all your laundry in front of you at your doorstep before issuing your booking receipt.
